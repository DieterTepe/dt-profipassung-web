Features

What DT-ProfiPassung calculates

For design, workshop, engineering offices, training and teaching – a single HTML file that calculates on your own device.

⌀

Quick entry as usual

Type the fit the way you write it, e.g. Ø50 H7/g6 – or pick nominal size and tolerance classes individually.

±

Deviations and limits of size

Upper and lower deviation, maximum and minimum size for hole and shaft, fundamental deviations A–ZC and a–zc, grades IT1–IT18.

⇔

Clearance, interference, fit type

Maximum and minimum clearance or interference and the classification as clearance, transition or interference fit.

⌁

Tolerance zones as a chart

Position of hole and shaft relative to the zero line – instantly visible where clearance or interference occurs.

✓

Self-checking calculation path

Formula, inserted values and source for each step. The program recalculates a second way and reports deviations.

◉

Press fits and practice

Interface pressure, transmissible torque and assembly temperature to DIN 7190, general tolerances to ISO 2768 and practical advice.

How it works

From input to result in a few steps

  • Enter the fit, for example Ø50 H7/g6.
  • If needed, choose press fit or general tolerance.
  • View the result and the tolerance zones.
  • Check the calculation path with its self-check.

FAQ

Questions about DT-ProfiPassung

Which standards does DT-ProfiPassung use?

Fits to ISO 286, press fits to DIN 7190 and general tolerances to ISO 2768.

What does the fit calculator provide?

Deviations, maximum and minimum sizes, clearance or interference, fit type and the tolerance zones as a chart.

How reliable are the results?

Each result is recalculated a second way and deviations are reported. Before binding use, please check against the original standard.

What does the free version do?

It calculates in full. Only outputs are locked: save, open, print and the Word report.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The free version runs directly in the browser, without sign-up or installation.

Does the program work offline?

Yes. All calculations run on your own device; no data is sent to any server.
