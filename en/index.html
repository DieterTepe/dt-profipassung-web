Worked examples

Calculate fits: H7/g6 and press fit H7/s6

Two typical workshop cases – a sliding fit and a shrink fit. Both examples are built into the program; all figures come from its calculation path to ISO 286 and DIN 7190.

Clearance fit H7/g6 – Ø 25 mm

The classic close sliding fit: the shaft is always smaller than the hole, so there is always a gap. The ISO 286 deviations give the limits of size and the clearance.

Hole 25 H7: ES = +21 µm, EI = 0 → 25.000 … 25.021 mm
Shaft 25 g6: es = −7 µm, ei = −20 µm → 24.980 … 24.993 mm
Minimum clearance = EI − es = 0 − (−7) = 7 µm
Maximum clearance = ES − ei = 21 − (−20) = 41 µm

Interference and press fit – shrink fit 60 H7/s6

Steel gear on a steel shaft Ø 60 mm, hub outside diameter 120 mm, joint length 50 mm, dry (µ ≈ 0.14), required torque 250 N·m. Following DIN 7190 the program calculates joint pressure, transmittable torque, press-in force and joining temperature.

Interference 23 … 72 µm
Joint pressure p = 24.3 … 88.6 N/mm² ≤ pzul = 153.7 N/mm²
Mt,max = 962.2 N·m → slip safety SH = 3.85
Press-in force Fe = 116.9 kN · heat hub to 211.3 °C

FAQ

Questions about DT-ProfiPassung

What does H7/g6 mean?

H7 is the tolerance zone of the hole (fundamental deviation H, lower deviation 0, tolerance grade IT7), g6 that of the shaft. Together they form a clearance fit: at Ø 25 mm the clearance is between 7 and 41 µm.

Which standards does DT-ProfiPassung use?

Fits to ISO 286, press fits to DIN 7190 and general tolerances to ISO 2768.

What does the fit calculator provide?

Deviations, maximum and minimum sizes, clearance or interference, fit type and the tolerance zones as a chart.

How reliable are the results?

Each result is recalculated a second way and deviations are reported. Before binding use, please check against the original standard.

What does the free version do?

It calculates in full. Only outputs are locked: save, open, print and the Word report.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The free version runs directly in the browser, without sign-up or installation.

Does the program work offline?

Yes. All calculations run on your own device; no data is sent to any server.
